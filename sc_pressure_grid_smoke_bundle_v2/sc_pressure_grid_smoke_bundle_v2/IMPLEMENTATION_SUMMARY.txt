SC LMCache pressure-grid smoke implementation, switch-enabled revision
======================================================================

Grid size: 16 one-factor-at-a-time Slurm jobs
Default MAX_QUESTIONS: 1
GNN: disabled throughout
Datasets: Medical baseline and HotpotQA alternate
Submission batch sizes: 50 baseline, 10, 250
vLLM max_num_seqs: 4 baseline, 12

Behavioral experiments:
- scheduler lookup admission: enabled default, max 1, max 12, wait 100 ms,
  wait 1000 ms
- worker lookup admission per TP rank: max 1, 4, 12
- disk-put admission: max pending 8, 1, 32

Fixed diagnostics enabled in the grid:
- SC_LMCACHE_IO_TRACE_ENABLE
- SC_LMCACHE_LOAD_TRACE_ENABLE
- SC_LMCACHE_MEMORY_TRACE_ENABLE
- SC_DRIVER_RESOURCE_MONITOR_ENABLE

Lightweight metric switch:
- The existing SC_LMCACHE_IO_TRACE_ENABLE switch also controls:
  [SC_DRIVER_REQUEST_METRIC]
  [SC_DRIVER_PHASE_SUMMARY]
  [SC_DRIVER_OUTPUT_CONSISTENCY]
  [SC_DRIVER_METRICS_DONE]
- Value 1 enables these records.
- Value 0 disables all four records.
- The pressure grid explicitly sets the switch to 1.
- The vanilla profile leaves it at 0.
- No additional environment variable was introduced.

Foreground/resume behavior:
- one Slurm job at a time
- 4-second interruptible warning before each protected submission section
- Ctrl-C outside the protected section stops only the master
- grid_state.json is authoritative and atomically updated
- active_job_id.txt is a best-effort managed mirror
- real failed/timed-out/cancelled jobs are recorded and not retried
- only a missing/untrackable active job record resubmits the unfinished config
- success requires Slurm COMPLETED and the === DONE === application marker
- a final ZIP archive is created after all 16 configurations are terminal

Apply compatibility:
- applies to the post-SC-refactor tree; or
- upgrades the immediately previous pressure-grid bundle in place.
